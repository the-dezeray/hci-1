Business Registration

Registering a Business Name: Requirements & Fee

Everything about registering a business name in one place: what to prepare, what it costs, and how to file.

info

Business name or company — which one do you need?

A business name is the simple option for a sole trader or small side business trading under its own name. A company (private, public or close) is a separate legal entity with shareholders and a compulsory constitution. If you plan to grow, employ staff or limit personal liability, read the private company guide instead.

What to prepare

Check these off before you start your application.

  • check_circleTwo or three proposed business names, in order of preference, in case your first choice is taken.
  • check_circleOwner details — full names, national ID or passport numbers, and contact details for each proprietor.
  • check_circleNature of the business — a short description of what the business will do.
  • check_circleBusiness address — physical location and postal address for correspondence.
  • check_circleRegistration fee — see the fee table below; payable when you submit.

For comparison, the constitution submission fee for companies is P500 (all company types).

How to file

Three steps from names to certificate.

  1. 1

    Search and reserve your name

    Check that your proposed name is available through OBRS Search the Register, then reserve it.

  2. 2

    Complete the application

    Fill in owner details, business description and address. If a form is required, get it from the Forms & Downloads library.

  3. 3

    Pay and submit via OBRS

    Pay the registration fee, submit, and keep your reference number and certificate copy.
